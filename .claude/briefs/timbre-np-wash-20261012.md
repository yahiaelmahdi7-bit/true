## PERFORMANCE RULE (we just fixed a freeze caused by this)
Do NOT draw a scaledToFill image + blur that grows to a huge layer. A blurred image behind a large area froze the whole app in WindowServer. Prefer palette gradients (LinearGradient/MeshGradient of 2-3 palette colours) over blurring the artwork. If you blur artwork, render it into a small fixed-size frame and scale the blurred result, never blur a page-sized layer.
